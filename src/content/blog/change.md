When I was a child, I was so resistant to change; I feel everything should not change the way it should be. Why? I was fine already. 

Well, that what I was thought. I was wrong. I needed change.

## Stagnant or Start?

Growing up made me realize **I needed change**. I remember barely alive in college when I decided to completely change my environment, and when how I was starting to feel that the decision was correct.

I shouldn't be *stagnant*, it is dangerous. Not only I will be left behind by the technology, I will be out of place with my friends or such. If I hate change now, I will surely lose all my friends since they are already on their own feet as they build their own future.

## Change it is

As much as I want it to be the same, change is inevitable.

Thank you.
